MEMOHONEY™ FORMULA & INGREDIENTS

Six Highlighted Ingredients in the MemoHoney™ Formula

MemoHoney™ combines six selected nutritional ingredients in a convenient daily formula. Explore the role of each ingredient and how the complete formula is positioned to support circulation, energy metabolism, focus, and everyday cognitive wellness.

01 Amino Acid

L-Arginine

L-Arginine is an amino acid used by the body in the production of nitric oxide, a molecule involved in normal blood vessel function. MemoHoney™ includes L-Arginine as part of its focused nutritional approach to circulation and everyday wellness.

Supports normal nitric-oxide pathways
02 Amino Acid Complex

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract combines L-Arginine with alpha-ketoglutarate in a commonly used supplemental form. It contributes to the formula's broader nutritional approach to circulation and daily wellness.

Complementary amino acid nutrition
03 Amino Acid

L-Citrulline HCL

L-Citrulline HCL is an amino acid that the body can convert into L-Arginine. It is included alongside other amino acids as part of MemoHoney™'s nutritional approach to normal nitric-oxide pathways and everyday wellness.

Supports amino acid pathways
04 Citrulline Compound

L-Citrulline Malate

L-Citrulline Malate combines citrulline with malate and is commonly used in nutritional and sports-focused formulas. MemoHoney™ includes it as part of its broader amino-acid and wellness formulation.

Citrulline and malate combination
05 Vitamin B3

Niacin Vitamin B3

Niacin, also known as vitamin B3, is an essential nutrient involved in normal energy-yielding metabolism. In MemoHoney™, it complements the formula's nutritional approach to everyday energy metabolism and wellness.

Supports normal energy metabolism
06 Amino Acid

Beta-Alanine

Beta-Alanine is a non-essential amino acid best known for its use in sports nutrition. MemoHoney™ includes it as one of six highlighted ingredients contributing to the formula's overall nutritional profile.

Selected amino acid nutrition
