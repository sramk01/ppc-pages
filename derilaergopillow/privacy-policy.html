Privacy Policy

At Derila Pillow™, protecting your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when visiting our website, purchasing our products, or interacting with our services. By accessing or using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, we may collect certain information to ensure a smooth and secure user experience.

  • Personal Information: When placing an order, subscribing to updates, or contacting customer support, we may collect details such as your name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping addresses, and order information.
  • Technical & Usage Data: We may automatically gather information including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and browsing behavior to improve website performance and functionality.

Payment information is securely processed by trusted third-party payment providers. Derila Pillow™ does not store complete credit card numbers or sensitive financial information on its servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect helps us provide a better customer experience and maintain our services.

  • Process and deliver orders
  • Provide customer service and respond to inquiries
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Share promotional offers or updates if you choose to receive them
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations

We respect your privacy and do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Website Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality, remember preferences, and analyze website traffic. You may adjust your browser settings to disable cookies, although some features of the website may not operate properly without them.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. While we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

In order to operate our website and fulfill orders, we may work with trusted third-party providers such as payment processors, shipping partners, analytics services, and customer support platforms. These partners receive only the information necessary to perform their services and are required to maintain appropriate privacy and security standards.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for deletion of your data. You may also opt out of promotional emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in our communications.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page, and continued use of the website indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact our customer support team. Derila Pillow™ is committed to maintaining a secure, transparent, and trustworthy online experience for all customers.
