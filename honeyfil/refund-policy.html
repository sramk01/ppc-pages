Last updated: August 10, 2026

Our Guarantee

We stand behind Honeyfil with a 60-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with your results, you may request a full refund of the product purchase price within 60 days of your original order date — even on empty bottles.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Email support@honey--fil.com with your order number within 60 days of purchase.
  2. Our support team will confirm your refund and provide return instructions if applicable.
  3. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-10 business days of approval.

What's Covered

The guarantee covers the purchase price of Honeyfil product(s). Original shipping fees, where applicable, are non-refundable.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a damaged or incorrect order, contact us within 14 days of delivery and we will arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost.

Questions
