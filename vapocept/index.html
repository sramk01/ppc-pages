L-Arginine

An amino acid that the body converts directly into nitric oxide—the molecule responsible for dilating blood vessels in the brain. With wider circulation pathways, more oxygen and nutrients reach your neurons, reducing those frustrating “blanks” when trying to recall a name, a word, or where you left your keys.

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract

An advanced form of arginine bound to alpha-ketoglutarate, which improves absorption and extends the vasodilating effect throughout the day. It acts as a slow-release fuel for brain tissue, sustaining focus and mental sharpness without energy spikes or crashes.

L-Citrulline HCL

A precursor that the body recycles back into arginine, creating a continuous cycle of nitric oxide production. This keeps cerebral blood vessels relaxed and blood flow steady even during moments of stress, fatigue, or poor sleep.

L-Citrulline Malate

Combines citrulline with malate, a compound that plays a direct role in energy production within cells. Beyond sustaining vasodilation, it helps eliminate metabolic waste that causes brain fatigue—that heavy-headed feeling at the end of the day.

Niacina

An essential vitamin for the production of NAD+, the molecule that powers the energy machinery inside every neuron. It also improves cerebral microcirculation, ensuring that all the nutrients in the formula reach exactly where they need to act—and don’t get lost along the way.

Beta-Alanine

An amino acid that raises carnosine levels in the brain, a natural antioxidant that protects neurons from age-related wear and tear. It works as a shield that preserves your brain cells from deterioration caused by time, stress, and demanding daily routines.
