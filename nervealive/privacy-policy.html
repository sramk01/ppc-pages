Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

At Nerve Alive, we value your privacy and strive to make your online experience enjoyable and secure. Here’s how we handle your information:

Personal Information We Collect and How We Use It

Registration: When you sign up for our products or services, we may ask for personal information such as your name, address, phone number, and billing details. This information is used to manage your account and process payments.

Partners and Sponsors: Sometimes, we offer products and services in partnership with other companies. In these cases, we may share your personal information with the partner to provide the service. Your information will not be used by the partner for any other purpose.

Online Shopping: When you make a purchase, we collect necessary information to process your order. We do not share this information with third parties except when necessary to complete your order.

Online Advertisements: We may display online advertisements and share aggregated, non-identifying information with advertisers. This helps us deliver tailored ads without sharing your personal information.

Email Inquiries: When you contact us via email, we use your email address only to respond to your inquiry. We do not share your email address with third parties.

Customer Surveys: We may conduct surveys to improve our services. Your responses are kept confidential, and aggregated data may be shared with third parties to enhance our offerings.

Promotions: If you participate in contests or sweepstakes, we may share your information with third parties to administer the promotion.

Special Cases: We may disclose personal information in special cases, such as to comply with laws, protect our company, or respond to legal requests.

California Privacy Rights: California residents have the right to know what personal information we share with third parties. To request this information, please email us with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line.

Cookies: We use cookies to improve your experience on our site. Cookies do not enable third parties to access your information. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Children’s Privacy: Our website is intended for users 18 years or older. We do not collect information from children under 18.

Public Forums: Be cautious when sharing personal information in public areas of our site, as this information becomes public.

Data Security: We have security measures in place to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee against unauthorized access.

Questions and Revisions: If you have questions about our privacy policy, please contact us. We reserve the right to revise this policy at any time.

Opting Out: To stop receiving emails from us, click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email or fill out the form below.
