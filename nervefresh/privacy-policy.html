Log Files
Nerve Fresh utilizes log files as a standard practice. These files record visitors when accessing websites, a common procedure among all hosting services for analytical purposes. Data in the log files comprises elements such as IP addresses, browser specifics, ISP details, timestamps, referral and exit pages, and even click counts. This data remains separate from any personal identifiers. The primary intent of such data collection is to discern patterns, manage the site effectively, monitor website navigation, and collate broad demographic insights.

Cookies and Web Beacons
Similar to many websites, Nerve Fresh employs 'cookies.' These cookies capture details like visitor preferences and the pages they explored on our site. This data aids in enhancing the user experience by tailoring our webpage content to align with the visitor's browser or other particulars.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie
Google operates as a third-party vendor on Nerve Fresh. It employs cookies, specifically DART cookies, to present ads to our visitors based on their past visits to Nerve Fresh and other online destinations. Nonetheless, users can opt-out of DART cookies by accessing Google's ad and content network Privacy Policy via this link: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
