Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Men’s Growth,

At Men’s Growth, accessible from this website, one of our top priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by Men’s Growth and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us via email.

Log Files

Men’s Growth follows standard procedures for using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting providers do this as part of their analytics services. Information collected by log files may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring or exit pages, and the number of clicks. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used to analyze trends, administer the site, track users’ movement, and gather demographic data.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many websites, Men’s Growth uses cookies to store information about visitors’ preferences and the pages they access or visit. This information helps us optimize the user experience by customizing website content based on visitors’ browser types or other relevant information.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our website and may use cookies, including DART cookies, to serve advertisements to visitors based on their visits to this and other websites. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting Google’s Advertising Privacy Policy.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

You may consult this section to find the Privacy Policy for each of Men’s Growth’s advertising partners. Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their ads and links that appear on Men’s Growth. These are sent directly to users’ browsers and automatically receive your IP address. These technologies are used to measure advertising effectiveness and personalize content.

Please note that Men’s Growth has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Men’s Growth’s Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to review the Privacy Policies of third-party ad servers or websites for more detailed information about their practices and instructions on how to opt out of certain options.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings. More detailed information about cookie management is available on your browser’s respective website.

Children’s Information

Protecting children while using the internet is especially important to us. Men’s Growth does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided such information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it from our records.

Online Privacy Policy Only

This Privacy Policy applies only to online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regard to information shared or collected on Men’s Growth. This policy does not apply to information collected offline or through channels other than this website.
