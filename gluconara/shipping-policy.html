Shipping Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days of purchase confirmation, excluding weekends and holidays.

Domestic Shipping (United States)

Standard domestic delivery typically takes 5–7 business days after dispatch. Free shipping is included on 3-bottle and 6-bottle package orders; a small shipping fee applies to single-bottle orders.

International Shipping

We ship to a number of international destinations. Delivery times vary by country and can range from 8–15 business days. Customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees imposed by their country.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email containing tracking information.

Shipping Delays

While we aim to meet the estimated delivery windows above, delays may occasionally occur due to carrier issues, customs processing, or high order volume during promotions.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Please ensure your shipping address is accurate at checkout. We are not responsible for delays or non-delivery resulting from incorrect address information provided by the customer.
