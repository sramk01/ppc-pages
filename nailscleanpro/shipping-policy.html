Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Shipping Policy outlines general timelines and practices for orders placed through the official NailsCleanPro checkout page linked from this Site.

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days of purchase. You will receive an email confirmation once your order has been placed and a second email with tracking information once it ships.

Estimated Delivery Times

  • Domestic orders: approximately 5–7 business days
  • International orders: approximately 10–20 business days, depending on destination and customs processing

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery times can be affected by carrier delays, weather, customs, or high order volume.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs, if any, are calculated and displayed at checkout on the official order page prior to payment confirmation.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking information to update after it's issued.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, contact us via our Contact page and we'll help you follow up with the shipping carrier.
