Shipping Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days of purchase confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

Standard domestic delivery typically takes 5–7 business days after processing. International shipping times vary by destination and customs processing.

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information so you can monitor delivery status.
