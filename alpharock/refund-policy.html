Refund Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

We want you to feel confident trying Alpha Rock. That's why every order is covered by our 60-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

1. 60-Day Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 60 days of your original purchase date to request a refund of the product purchase price.

2. How to Request a Refund

  • Email support@alpharock.store with your order number and request.
  • Our support team will confirm eligibility and provide return instructions, if applicable.

3. What's Covered

The guarantee applies to the product purchase price. Original shipping charges, where applicable, are non-refundable.

If your order arrived damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 14 days of delivery for a replacement or refund at no additional cost.

5. Subscription Cancellations

If you are enrolled in a recurring subscription order, you may cancel at any time before your next billing date by contacting support.
