Shipping Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-20

This Shipping Policy explains how FlowStrong processes, ships, and delivers orders, so you know what to expect after you check out.

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days of purchase. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

Delivery Times

Domestic delivery generally takes 3-10 business days depending on your location and the shipping method selected at checkout. International delivery times vary by destination and customs processing, and can take longer.

These are estimates, not guarantees — delays can occur due to weather, carrier issues, or high order volume that are outside our control.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number so you can follow your package's progress to your door.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs, if any, are calculated and displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase. Any promotional free shipping offers will be clearly noted on the order page.

Incorrect Addresses

Please double-check your shipping address before completing your order. We are not responsible for packages delayed or lost due to an incorrect address provided at checkout.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged or does not arrive within the expected timeframe, contact us and we'll work with the carrier to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

International Orders

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees charged by their country, which are not included in the order total.

Contact Us

Questions about shipping can be directed to support@FlowStrong.com.
