L-Arginine

L-Arginine is a naturally occurring amino acid that serves as a precursor to nitric oxide, a compound that helps support healthy blood vessel function and normal circulation. It is widely used in wellness supplements to promote healthy blood flow and support overall cardiovascular performance as part of a balanced lifestyle.

🧠 Memory & Cognitive Learning Support

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract

L-Arginine Alpha-Ketoglutarate (AKG) combines L-Arginine with alpha-ketoglutarate to provide an enhanced form of this important amino acid. It is commonly included in advanced nutritional formulas to support healthy nitric oxide production, circulation, and sustained nutrient delivery throughout the body.

🎯 Stress Balance & Mental Focus

L-Citrulline HCL

L-Citrulline HCL is an amino acid that is naturally converted into L-Arginine within the body. This process helps support healthy nitric oxide levels, promotes normal blood flow, and contributes to overall vascular wellness. It is frequently used in performance and wellness supplements to complement healthy circulation.

🌿 Calm Focus & Mental Clarity

L-Citrulline Malate

L-Citrulline Malate combines L-Citrulline with malic acid, creating a well-known ingredient used to support healthy circulation and cellular energy metabolism. This combination is valued for helping maintain endurance, supporting energy production, and promoting overall physical performance during everyday activities.

🔥 Mental Energy & Vitality Support

Niacin (Vitamin B3)

Niacin is an essential B vitamin that plays a vital role in converting food into usable energy. It supports healthy cellular metabolism, nervous system function, and normal circulation while contributing to overall energy production. Niacin is an important nutrient for maintaining daily vitality and overall wellness.

🔥 Cellular Energy Support

Beta-Alanine

Beta-Alanine is a naturally occurring amino acid commonly included in wellness and performance supplements. It helps support healthy muscle function, physical endurance, and everyday stamina by contributing to the body's natural production of carnosine. Beta-Alanine also complements an active lifestyle by supporting sustained physical and mental performance.
