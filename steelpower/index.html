L-Citrulline is a naturally occurring amino acid that helps support the body's nitric oxide production. Healthy nitric oxide levels help maintain healthy blood flow and circulation, supporting exercise performance, endurance, and overall cardiovascular wellness.

L-Carnitine

L-Carnitine plays an important role in healthy energy metabolism by helping transport fatty acids into the mitochondria where they can be converted into usable energy. It is widely used to support physical performance, endurance, and post-workout recovery.

Pine Bark Extract

Pine Bark Extract is rich in naturally occurring antioxidants that help protect cells from oxidative stress while supporting healthy circulation and vascular function. It complements the formula by promoting overall cardiovascular wellness.

Maca Root Extract

Panax Ginseng
