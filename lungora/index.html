The Natural Ingredients in Lungora

Magnesium Glycinate

Magnesium Glycinate is a highly absorbable form of magnesium that supports normal nerve and muscle function. It also plays a role in energy metabolism and electrolyte balance, making it a useful nutritional addition for adults focused on everyday wellness, mobility, and overall physical function.

Alpha Lipoic Acid

Alpha Lipoic Acid is a naturally occurring antioxidant compound that helps support the body's existing antioxidant defenses. It's included in Lungora to offer nutritional support for cellular wellness and to help maintain healthy energy processes as part of a balanced daily routine.

Butcher’s Broom Root Extract

Butcher's Broom Root Extract is a botanical traditionally used to support healthy circulation and vascular wellness. Lungora includes this plant-based extract as part of its broader approach to supporting comfortable everyday activity, healthy circulation, and general wellness in adults.

L-Carnitine

L-Carnitine is a naturally occurring compound that helps transport fatty acids into cells, where they're converted into usable energy. By supporting normal cellular energy metabolism, L-Carnitine rounds out Lungora's formula for adults seeking daily vitality and support for an active lifestyle.

Turmeric Extract

Turmeric Extract contains naturally occurring compounds recognized for their antioxidant properties. It's included in Lungora to provide nutritional support for cellular wellness and the body's natural defense against everyday oxidative stress, complementing the formula's broader wellness and healthy-aging goals.

Coenzyme Q10

Coenzyme Q10, better known as CoQ10, is a naturally occurring compound involved in cellular energy production. It also lends antioxidant support, rounding out Lungora's nutritional approach to cellular wellness, everyday energy, and overall vitality as part of a healthy lifestyle.
