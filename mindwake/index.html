01

Ginkgo Biloba (Leaf Extract)

A botanical extract traditionally valued for its role in supporting healthy circulation and cognitive performance, making it a thoughtful addition to a daily cognitive wellness formula.

02

Phosphatidylserine

A naturally occurring phospholipid that provides nutritional support for memory, learning, attention, and healthy cognitive function.

03

N-Acetyl L-Carnitine HCI

A form of L-carnitine involved in cellular energy metabolism that provides nutritional support for mental energy, focus, and everyday cognitive performance.

04

Bacopa Monnieri (Leaf Extract)

A botanical ingredient traditionally used in wellness practices and selected for its role in supporting memory, learning, and healthy cognitive function.

05

L-Glutamine

An amino acid involved in normal cellular and neurotransmitter processes that helps provide nutritional support for healthy brain communication and cognitive wellness.

06

Alpha GPC (Alpha-Glycerylphosphorylcholine)

A choline-containing compound that supports acetylcholine production, an important neurotransmitter involved in memory, learning, attention, and normal cognitive processes.
