BHB is a naturally occurring ketone that provides an alternative source of energy for the body. As an exogenous ketone, it can help support energy production while following a reduced-carbohydrate eating plan and may assist the body's natural fat metabolism. BHB is often used to help users maintain focus, energy, and consistency throughout their wellness journey.
